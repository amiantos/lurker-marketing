About Lurker

A modern, always-on IRC client and bouncer — open source, self-hostable, and run as a hosted service at lurker.chat.

1.0What it is

Lurker keeps you present on IRC. It holds your connections open, records what happens in your channels while you're away, and hands it back through a clean, fast interface that works the same in any web browser or mobile phone.

2.0Why it exists

Personally, I got tired of using a centralized chat service that was constantly shoving advertisements and requests for money into my face. So, I came back to IRC, but none of the clients I tried left me fully satisfied. I wanted a self-hosted client with the power and flexibility of a terminal client, and the ease of use and conveniences of a modern application, that also looked really, really easy on the eyes. I had no choice: I started working on Lurker for myself. As I showed it to other people on IRC, more and more of them wanted to use it themselves. Thanks to the enthusiasm of other IRC users, Lurker has grown into a fully featured IRC bouncer and client system.

3.0Philosophy

I believe deeply in the philosophy of open source software, which is why I wanted to make a production-quality cloud-hosted IRC bouncer and client that is fully open source for anyone to use. I also wanted Lurker to be a first-class IRC client, without the bloat and unnecessary 'modern' features of other IRC clients.

Lurker is not trying to be an ecosystem, or recreate Discord with IRC: it is just a really good IRC client.
