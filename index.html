02  /  Overview

Presence confirmed.

Old IRC went dark the moment you closed the window. Lurker doesn't. It holds your connection open, captures everything said while you were away, and hands it back the next time you look — on any web browser. A clean modern interface in support of a protocol that has outlasted everything sent to replace it.

Always connected

Lurker keeps you connected, even if your own internet connection can be flaky.

A·2

Full history

Whether you're online or away, Lurker captures everything said in your channels and makes it available when you return.

A·3

Modern client

A fast web app bringing modern features to IRC — search, uploads, notifications, notes, drafts, smart nick autocomplete, and more.
